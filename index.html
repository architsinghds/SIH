A Little Letter For You

Written with love, sealed with a kiss.

My Dearest Love,

Meghana, my Bangaram ❤️ I know this might sound a little stupid or filmy, but you genuinely are the best thing that has ever happened to me. I never imagined I could be this happy, this loved, or this emotionally attached to someone. Tumse milne se pehle, I was just a random guy living his life without any real dreams, ambitions, or plans for the future. I was just going with the flow, not knowing what I actually wanted from life. Par tumhare aane ke baad sab kuch badal gaya. Ab mere paas dreams hain, ambitions hain, aur sabse important, ek beautiful future hai jiske liye main har din mehnat karna chahta hoon. And the best part? Jab bhi main apne future ke baare mein sochta hoon, har picture mein tum hoti ho. I imagine celebrating my smallest achievements with you, sharing my happiest moments with you, fighting over silly things, laughing over stupid jokes, growing old together, and making a lifetime of memories with you. I don't just want you in my happy moments. I want you beside me when life gets difficult, when things don't go as planned, and when everything feels a little too much. I want us to choose each other, even on the days when loving each other takes a little extra effort. Sach kahun toh, tumhare bina apne future ki imagination bhi nahi kar pata. I don't want a life where I have to look for someone else to share all these little things with. It's you, Meghana. It has always been you, and I hope it will always be you. I have given you a part of my heart that I never knew existed. You've made me experience a kind of happiness and love that I never knew I was capable of feeling. And I don't want this to be just a beautiful chapter of my life. I want you to be my entire story. So, today, I have just one little request. Can you promise me that you'll keep choosing me? That no matter how many years pass, how much life changes, or how many ups and downs we face, we'll never stop trying for each other? I don't expect us to be perfect. I don't expect us to never fight or disagree. I just want a love where we never give up on each other, where we communicate, understand, forgive, and keep falling in love with each other over and over again. I want a real promise, not just words said in a beautiful moment. A promise that you'll genuinely try to keep, just like I promise to do the same. I want to be yours, not just today, not just tomorrow, but for all the tomorrows that life has waiting for us. So, Meghana, my Bangaram... Will you promise me that we'll always be us? That you'll keep holding my hand, and let me hold yours, through every chapter of our lives?

Forever Yours,
Your Love ❤️
